About Us

Who Are We?

The SLIET Antriksha Vigyan Club is a passionate collective of students, researchers, and astronomy enthusiasts at Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering and Technology. We bridge theoretical physics with practical space technology, driving curiosity through telescopes, satellite systems, computational astrophysics, and outreach programs.

Vision & Objectives

Our primary vision is to establish SLIET as a hub for student-led space innovations, foster hands-on learning with advanced optical instrumentation, enable satellite design projects, and inspire the upcoming generation to explore cosmic phenomena.

Specialized Wings

Our Operational Domains

Explore the different sub-fields our club members specialize in and drive projects forward.

Astronomy & Observation

Hands-on night sky observations, celestial object tracking, planetary alignment sessions, and optical telescope maintenance.

Astrophysics & Space Science

Theoretical research into stellar evolution, dark matter, cosmology, gravitational mechanics, and astrophysical calculations.

Space Tech & Computing

Software simulation for orbital trajectories, astronomical data analysis using Python, and specialized web tools.

Satellite & Electronics

CanSat prototypes, ground station receiver setup, CubeSat communication modules, and embedded hardware systems.

Astrophotography & Outreach

Capturing long-exposure deep space imagery, organizing campus workshops, school outreach, and public stargazing drives.
